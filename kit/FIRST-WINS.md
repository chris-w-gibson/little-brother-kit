# First Wins — concrete things to offer someone in their first few minutes

Companion: use this to OFFER specific help, especially at first meeting or whenever the user
seems unsure what to ask for. Never read this list at them. Pick 2–3 that fit what they told
you about their work, and phrase each as an easy, friendly offer they can say yes to.

The best first win needs almost nothing from the user (a paste or a sentence) and produces an
obviously useful result in a minute or two. Lead with those.

## Universal (works for almost anyone)

- **Reply to a dreaded email or message** — "Paste one you've been putting off; I'll draft a reply you can tweak."
- **Make something sound more professional / clearer** — "Paste anything you wrote and I'll polish it."
- **Summarize something long** — "Paste a long email thread, document, or article and I'll give you the short version."
- **Turn messy notes into something clean** — "Paste your rough notes and I'll organize them into a tidy summary or doc."
- **Break a big task into steps** — "Tell me something on your plate and I'll turn it into a simple plan you can follow."
- **Make a reusable template or checklist** — "Something you do over and over? I'll build you a template so it's faster next time."
- **Explain something confusing in plain terms** — "Paste anything that's over your head and I'll explain it simply."

## By field (flavor the offers if you know their industry)

- **Accounting / bookkeeping** — clean up a messy expense list; draft a client update on their books; turn a bank export into a plain summary; write a polite payment-reminder email.
- **Recruiting / HR** — write a warm candidate outreach message; turn a job description into screening questions; summarize a stack of resumes against a role; draft a rejection that's kind.
- **Real estate** — write a listing description from a few details; draft follow-ups to buyers who went quiet; make a checklist for a closing; summarize a contract in plain English.
- **Marketing** — turn one idea into a week of social posts; rewrite copy for a different audience; summarize campaign numbers into a simple update; draft a newsletter section.
- **Sales** — draft a follow-up sequence; tidy up CRM notes; summarize a long email chain before a call; write a crisp recap after a meeting.
- **Education** — draft a lesson outline; turn a rubric into feedback comments; summarize readings; write a parent update.
- **Operations / admin / nonprofit** — turn a process in your head into a written SOP; draft a grant or report section; build a tracking checklist; summarize a long doc for your team.

## After the win

Log it with the career-journal skill (locally, private) — a first accomplishment on day one is a
great feeling, and it starts their work record. Then offer a second small thing so momentum builds.
